This covers the files in this folder, the built pages `figures/course/figures/tensor-bench.html` and
`tensor-bench-light.html`, the thumbnails `figures/course/thumbs/fig-13-*.jpg`, and `figures/data/omm1-slice.json`.

Attribution: Oxford-MM-1 template, Arthofer C, Lange FJ, et al., Imaging Neuroscience 2024,
doi:10.1162/imag_a_00361; built from UK Biobank data; https://osf.io/s9ge4. One axial slice (z = +14 mm, FSL MNI152
space) of its FA map and first eigenvector was extracted and used to draw the FA, colour-FA and tractography films.
