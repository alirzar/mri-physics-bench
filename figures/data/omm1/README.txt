Oxford-MM-1 (OMM-1) diffusion tensor template: FA and first eigenvector (V1)

Files
  OMM-1_DTI_FA.nii.gz   fractional anisotropy, 182 x 218 x 182, 1 mm
  OMM-1_DTI_V1.nii.gz   principal diffusion direction (first eigenvector), 182 x 218 x 182 x 3, 1 mm

Source
  Oxford-MM-1 template project on OSF: https://osf.io/s9ge4 (data DOI 10.17605/OSF.IO/S9GE4)
  Downloaded 2026-11-06 from https://osf.io/download/zaymc/ (FA) and https://osf.io/download/6dcyk/ (V1).

Reference
  Arthofer C, Lange FJ, et al. Imaging Neuroscience 2024. doi:10.1162/imag_a_00361
  The template was built from UK Biobank data.
